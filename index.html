DocketFlag
Docket watch for solo & small-firm attorneys

The filing you're waiting for posts at 4:47 PM. You find out tomorrow.

Opposing counsel files, judges issue orders, deadlines start running - and the only way to know is to keep checking the docket. DocketFlag checks it for you, several times a day, and sends a sourced flag the day anything new posts on the cases you name.

How it works

  1. Name the matter. A case number, a party name, or an opposing attorney. Federal courts today; tell us if you need a state system and we'll tell you honestly whether we can watch it.
  2. We pull the docket, repeatedly. Several times a day, every day. Not a weekly digest, not a monthly sweep.
  3. You get the flag. Document number, what was filed, who filed it, the date, and the link to the docket so you can pull the document yourself.
